      'Mass' is the total stellar baryonic mass (Msun),
      'lg_Mdot' is log10 magnitude of rate of change of mass (Msun/year),
      'lg_Dsurf' is log10 surface density (g/cm^3),
      'H_env' is the amount of mass where H is dominant,
